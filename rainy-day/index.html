a zine for staying in
instructions
for a
rainy
day.
first
watch the window fog
from the inside
count the drops racing
each other down the glass
put the kettle on
two times,
maybe three
then
stomp through every
puddle on the way to
nowhere in particular
listen to the gutters
arguing with themselves
all afternoon
and remember
let your bones remember
that somewhere under
all this rain
are valleys worn
by wind and water
and this water has
fallen before,
these same atoms rising
and falling
and rising again
the rain always
comes back
to remind you
you’re still allowed
to be astonished
by something as ordinary
as water
falling from the sky
again
and again
rain log
date:
what it sounded like:
what it smelled like:
where i watched it from:
what i did instead:
my rainy
day list
it will
pass.
italwayscomesback.
